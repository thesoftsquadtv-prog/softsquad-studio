**Skit lessons (21 Nov, apply to Thanksgiving 24 Nov):**
- Keep the food character's motion small and slow on screen (eyes, wobble, a slow lean or roll). Fast action (falling, being grabbed or lifted) makes Omni redesign the face. Imply the fast moments off-screen: a cut, a sound, or the human's reaction.
